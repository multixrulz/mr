Project Hail Mary

A number of people told me that I would enjoy watching Project Hail Mary. A long haul flight seemed like a great time to watch the movie.

I really did enjoy the film. As SciFi goes, this one didn’t commit too many wrong-science sins, and the story was very engaging.
