Another long haul flight and another space movie. This time I watched The Martian. Mark Watney gets stranded on Mars and has to keep himself alive long enough to be rescued. Like Project Hail Mary, this movie also celebrates ingenuity and clever hacks. There was really nothing to complain about physics-wise, but it was a bit annoying to see a couple of times that smart people overlooked obvious problems so that they could be discovered later and keep the plot exciting. However, that didn’t detract from a thoroughly gripping SciFi movie that I fully recommend.
