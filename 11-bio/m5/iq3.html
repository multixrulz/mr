Year 11 Biology Module 5

Inquiry Question 3

Why is polypeptide synthesis important?

Facts

I can

  • Define
    • peptide
    • polypeptide
    • protein
  • Define “expresssion” of a gene
  • Describe and explain the differences between DNA, mRNA, tRNA and rRNA
  • Describe transcription
    • initiation
    • elongation
    • termination
  • Describe and explain the differences between triplets, codons and anticodons
  • Describe translation - how ribosomes create proteins
    • initiation
    • elongation
    • termination
  • Define
    • start codon
    • stop codon
    • intron
    • extron
    • promoter
  • Describe the differences between prokaryotic and eukaryotic protein synthesis

Skills

I can

  • Transcribe DNA to RNA
  • Translate codons to amino acids

Concepts

I can

  • Explain the purpose of transcription and translation
  • Explain the meaning of degeneracy of DNA code
  • Explain why folding is important for proteins
