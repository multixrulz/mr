Year 11 Biology Module 5

Inquiry Question 2

How important is it for genetic material to be replicated exactly?

Facts

I can

  • State 3 reasons for cell replication.
  • Draw the cell cycle.
  • Describe what happens in each stage of the cell cycle.
  • Describe mitosis and meiosis, including drawings and phase names.
  • Describe how genetic variation is introduced by mieosis.
  • Define DNA terms (nucleic acid, nucleotide, base, sugar-phosphate backbone, codon, chromatin, chromatid, chromosome) and identify them in diagrams.
  • Identify 3’ and 5’ ends of DNA.
  • Define gene, allele, and genome and identify where they physically exist.
  • Describe the process of DNA replication.
  • Identify different ways that DNA replication errors and DNA damage can occur.
  • Define somatic mutation and germline mutation.
  • Describe what cells do in response to DNA replication errors.

Concepts

I can

  • Explain the reason for each phase of mitosis and meiosis.
  • Explain why genetic variation is important.
  • Explain how a DNA molecule is composed of smaller units and how H-bonding and condensation polymerisation hold it together.
  • Explain the chemistry origin of the names 3’ and 5’ for the ends of DNA strands.
  • Explain the effects of innaccurate DNA replication and why DNA replication needs to be accurate.
  • Explain the different results of somatic and germline mutations.
