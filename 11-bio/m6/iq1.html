Year 11 Biology Module 6

Inquiry Question 1

Inquiry question: How does mutation introduce new alleles into a population?

Facts

I can

  • Recall 3 mutagen types (EM radiation, chemicals, natural), and examples of each type.
  • Describe silent, missense, nonsense and frameshift mutations.
  • Describe the possible results of mutation in introns and other non-coding DNA.
  • Describe types of chromosomal mutation (duplication, inversion, deletion, insertion, translocation).
  • Define somatic and germline mutation.
  • Describe sources of genetic variation in the processes of meiosis and fertilisation.
  • Define gene pool, gene flow and genetic drift.

Concepts

I can

  • Explain how each mutagen type (EM radiation, chemicals, natural) causes mutation.
  • Explain how silent, missense, nonsense and frameshift mutations affect polypeptid.e synthesis
  • Explain how mutation produces new alleles.
